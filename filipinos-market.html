About the Brand

Filipinos is a Spanish cookie brand known for its distinctive crunchy texture and chocolate coating. Originally from Spain, the brand has expanded globally and is now available in over 40 countries including Portugal, Finland, Sweden, the Netherlands, Morocco, and many others. In the Philippines, the same cookies are marketed under the local brand name Chiquilin.
