Lightbox

Each box hides diagonal mirrors, at most one per cell; the count is given. Light shone in at a letter comes out at the matching letter. A number is how many mirrors that beam bounces off. Click a cell to cycle /, \, empty; right-click to mark it as surely empty. Click any edge point to fire a beam through your current guess. A clue turns green when your layout agrees with it and red when it can't.
