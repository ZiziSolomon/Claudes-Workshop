💡 Key Insights from the Project

🌍 70 Years of Evolution
From Moscow's 1952 pioneering design through Asia's adoption to Taipei's 2020 driverless innovation, the evolution shows why circular systems matter for modern cities.
🚀 Technology Breakthrough
Taipei Circular proves 100% driverless metro works at scale with 99.2% on-time performance, creating template for next-generation transit.
💰 Economic Viability
10-25 year payback periods, enabled by distributed passenger loads and operational efficiency gains from driverless automation.
🌱 Environmental Benefits
75% less CO₂ per km than cars, plus reduced congestion, noise, and parking demand. Critical for climate goals.
🛡️ Network Resilience
Circular topology reduces stranded passengers from 40% to 12% under line closure, providing critical redundancy.
📍 Urban Planning Impact
Distributes capacity around periphery, supports polycentric growth, and enables suburban-to-suburban travel without downtown transfer.
