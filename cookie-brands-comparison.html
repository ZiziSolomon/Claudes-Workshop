What Makes a Cookie Brand Go Global?

Four success factors emerge:
1. Distinctive identity: Filipinos' signature crunchy-chocolate combo sets it apart
2. Cultural integration: Renamed as Chiquilin in Philippines shows local respect
3. Parent company scale: Artiach's resources enable 40+ country distribution
4. Heritage + consistency: Brands trusted for decades expand through reputation, not trend-chasing

Filipinos exemplifies this: Spanish heritage + consistent quality + smart regional adaptation = global staple.
