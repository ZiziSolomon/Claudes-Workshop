## Key Insights

✓ **Circular lines solve a different problem** — suburb-to-suburb, not downtown-centric  
✓ **Proven concept** — 70 years of precedent, 5 cities operating successfully  
✓ **Driverless is ideal** — Taipei proves 100% automation works at scale  
✓ **Economically viable** — 10-25 year payback with 200k+ daily riders  
✓ **Network benefits** — Reduces stranded passengers from 40% to 12%  
✓ **Environmental impact** — 75% less CO₂ than cars  
✓ **Predictable design** — 1.2-1.6 km station spacing, 15-50 km circumference  
✓ **Documented pitfalls** — 18 common mistakes identified and solutions provided  
✓ **Global trend** — Seoul, Shenzhen, Bangkok studying circular lines
