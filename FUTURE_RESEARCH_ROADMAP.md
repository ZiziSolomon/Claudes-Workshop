## Tier 1: Natural Extensions (High Priority)

These extend existing research in natural directions using similar methodology.

### 1A: Higher-Dimensional Attractors (4D, 5D)

**What:** Extend continuous chaos research to 4D and 5D systems

**Systems to explore:**
- 4D Rabinovich-Fabrikant attractor
- 5D coupled oscillator systems
- High-dimensional periodic orbits

**Methods:**
- 3D projection/slicing for visualization
- Sonification of higher dimensions (map to spatial audio)
- Analysis of eigenvalue structure in high dimensions

**Expected findings:**
- Does golden ratio persist in 4D/5D eigenvalues?
- How does complexity scale with dimensionality?
- What happens to emergence in higher dimensions?

**Time estimate:** 40-60 minutes (implementation + visualization)

**Value:** Tests whether structure persists beyond 3D

---

### 1B: More Cellular Automata (Rule 30, Others)

**What:** Complete the cellular automata survey with other rules

**Rules to explore:**
- Rule 30 (chaotic, often cited as "random")
- Rule 110 neighbors (compare to Rule 110)
- Totalistic CA (all neighborhood cells equivalent)
- 2D alternatives to Life (HighLife, Seeds, etc.)

**Methods:**
- 100,000+ generation evolution
- Classification by Wolfram class
- Sonification and visualization
- Analysis of emergence types

**Expected findings:**
- Do other Class IV rules show similar edge-of-chaos behavior?
- Are there emergence types beyond the three identified?
- How universal is the emergence principle?

**Time estimate:** 60-90 minutes (3-4 new CA implementations)

**Value:** Determines if emergence principle is truly universal

---

### 1C: Coupled Systems and Networks

**What:** Explore bifurcations in coupled oscillators

**Systems:**
- Two coupled logistic maps
- Network of coupled oscillators
- Mean-field bifurcations in networks

**Methods:**
- Bifurcation diagrams for coupled systems
- Synchronization phenomena
- Emergent rhythms and patterns

**Expected findings:**
- Does Feigenbaum δ persist in coupled systems?
- What new bifurcation phenomena emerge?
- How does coupling affect chaos?

**Time estimate:** 60-90 minutes

**Value:** Links to real-world phenomena (neural networks, oscillating circuits)

---

### 1D: Langton's Ant Variants

**What:** Explore variations on the ant rule

**Variants:**
- Multiple ants on same grid
- Ants with different rules
- Ants on different topologies (torus, non-Euclidean)

**Expected findings:**
- Do highways still emerge with multiple ants?
- What patterns emerge from ant interactions?
- How does topology affect emergence?

**Time estimate:** 40-60 minutes

**Value:** Tests if emergence is robust to rule variations

## Tier 2: Theoretical Extensions (Medium Priority)

These deepen understanding of the mathematical structures discovered.

### 2A: Renormalization Groups

**What:** Link scales mathematically

**Approach:**
- Study renormalization in bifurcation cascades
- Block-spin transformations for cellular automata
- Scaling relations near critical points

**Expected findings:**
- Is Feigenbaum δ a fixed point of renormalization?
- What does renormalization reveal about universality?
- How do scales couple mathematically?

**Value:** Connects scales theoretically, not just empirically

### 2B: Category Theory Perspective

**What:** View all structures as morphisms in a category

**Approach:**
- Represent cellular automata as morphisms
- Represent bifurcations as natural transformations
- Study functor relationships between categories

**Expected findings:**
- Do universal structures correspond to natural transformations?
- Is there a category of all dynamical systems?
- What structure is preserved by categorical functors?

**Value:** Reveals deeper mathematical unity

---

### 2C: Algebraic Topology Connection

**What:** Use homology and cohomology to analyze attractors

**Approach:**
- Compute Betti numbers for attractors
- Persistent homology of orbits
- Topological entropy from homological perspective

**Expected findings:**
- Can topological entropy be computed from homology?
- What do Betti numbers reveal about attractor structure?
- Is there universal homological structure?

**Value:** Links topology to dynamics algebraically

---

### 2D: Information-Theoretic Analysis

**What:** Deeper analysis of information production

**Approach:**

**Expected findings:**
- Is maximum information production at edge of chaos?
- What's the information cost of emergence?
- How does information flow relate to universality?

**Value:** Quantifies information aspects of structure
