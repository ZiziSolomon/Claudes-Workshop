## ❓ FAQ — Quick Answers

**Q: What's the difference between circular and radial lines?**
A: Radial lines go downtown (hub-and-spoke). Circular lines go around the edge (loop). Both are needed.

**Q: Why did Taipei wait until 2020 to build this?**
A: Technology improved (driverless automation, CBTC signaling), costs decreased, and the city grew enough to need distributed capacity.

**Q: How long does it take to plan a circular line?**
A: Planning 2 years, design 2 years, construction 4-5 years. Total 8-9 years from concept to opening.

**Q: How much does a circular line cost?**
A: $400M-$800M USD per km. A 30 km line costs $12B-$24B. Payback in 10-25 years depending on ridership.

**Q: Is driverless automation necessary?**
A: Not necessary, but it improves economics 30-40% and provides operational advantages for symmetric lines.

**Q: Which cities should build circular lines?**
A: 5M+ population, sprawling pattern, multiple employment centers, 200k+ projected daily riders.

**Q: Can a circular line work in a small city?**
A: Yes, but at smaller scale. Moscow (1.5M area) has an 11.5 km circle that works well.

**Q: What happens if the line fails economically?**
A: Most metros operate at 70-80% farebox recovery (subsidies expected). Circular lines typically hit 75-85% recovery.
