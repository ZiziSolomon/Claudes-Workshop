# Project Success Metrics & Performance Monitoring
## Measuring and Tracking Circular Metro Project Success

---

## Introduction

How do you know if your circular metro project is succeeding? Success isn't just "the trains run." It's achieving financial targets, operational goals, and broader city objectives.

This framework defines:
1. **Success metrics** for each project phase
2. **Performance indicators** for ongoing monitoring
3. **Benchmarks** from peer cities
4. **Reporting structure** for accountability

---

## Part 1: Success Phases & Metrics

### Phase 1: Planning & Feasibility (Years 0-2)

**Success = Project approved and funded at defined level**

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|---|
| **Political approval** | ✓ Council/parliament votes yes | Formal vote recorded |
| **Funding secured** | 60%+ of capital cost | Signed loan agreements, budget allocations |
| **Design progress** | 30%+ complete | Design review milestones |
| **Environmental approval** | ✓ Permits issued | Formal approval from agencies |
| **Community support** | 50%+ of surveyed support project | Opinion polls, public engagement feedback |
| **Cost estimate accuracy** | Within ±15% of peer cities | Cost comparison audit |
| **Schedule approval** | Realistic timeline established | Expert review of schedule |

**Success indicator:** At end of Phase 1, have signed loan agreements for 60%+ of cost and formal government approval.

---

### Phase 2: Design & Procurement (Years 2-4)

**Success = Design complete and construction contracts signed**

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|---|
| **Design completion** | 90%+ complete | Design review milestone |
| **Permits secured** | All major permits issued | Formal permit documentation |
| **Procurement process** | 70%+ of contracts bid | Contract awards tracked |
| **Cost updates** | Actual cost within ±10% of estimate | Updated cost forecast |
| **Schedule performance** | On schedule or early | Actual vs. planned milestones |
| **Stakeholder engagement** | Quarterly briefings complete | Meeting records |
| **Land acquisition** | 80%+ of required land secured | Property titles transferred |

**Success indicator:** At end of Phase 2, have all major construction contracts signed and 80%+ of funding confirmed (before breaking ground).

**Success = Infrastructure completed on budget and schedule**

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|---|

| **Schedule variance** | ≤3 months delay | Monthly schedule tracking |
| **Safety record** | <1 serious incident per 1M labor-hours | Accident reports |

**Success indicator:** At end of Phase 3, infrastructure complete and ready for systems integration testing.

## Part 2: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Operational KPIs

**On-Time Performance**
- **Definition:** % of trains departing/arriving within ±3 minutes of schedule
- **Target:** 95% minimum, 98%+ ideal
- **Measurement:** Automatic train control system data
- **Frequency:** Daily reporting, monthly summary
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (99.2%), Singapore (98%+), Tokyo (96%+)

**System Availability**
- **Definition:** % of scheduled service actually operated (not cancelled)
- **Target:** 99%+ (only 1-2 days/year unscheduled)
- **Measurement:** Automated dispatch system
- **Frequency:** Daily reporting
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (99.2%), most mature metros (98-99%)

**Average Passenger Satisfaction**
- **Definition:** % rating service 4+ on 5-point scale
- **Target:** ≥75% (Year 1-2), ≥80% (mature)
- **Measurement:** Annual passenger survey (minimum 1,000 respondents)
- **Frequency:** Quarterly surveys
- **Benchmark:** Tokyo (85%), Singapore (80%), Taipei (82%)

**Crowding Index (Load Factor)**
- **Definition:** Average % of capacity during peak hour
- **Target:** 70-80% (comfortable, not overcrowded)
- **Measurement:** Passenger counting system
- **Frequency:** Daily during peak hours
- **Benchmark:** Most metros 75-85% peak

**Safety Record**
- **Definition:** Serious injuries and fatalities per 1M passengers
- **Target:** <0.1 injuries per 1M passengers (near zero)
- **Measurement:** Incident reporting system
- **Frequency:** Monthly aggregate
- **Benchmark:** Driverless metros zero fatalities, <0.05 injuries per 1M

---

### Financial KPIs

**Farebox Revenue Ratio**
- **Definition:** Annual fare revenue ÷ Annual operating cost
- **Target:** ≥85% (Year 1-2), ≥100% (mature)
- **Measurement:** Financial accounting system
- **Frequency:** Monthly tracking, annual reporting
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (150%), Beijing (90%), average metro (75-80%)

**Cost per Passenger-km**
- **Definition:** Annual operating cost ÷ Total passenger-km
- **Target:** $0.03-0.05 per km
- **Measurement:** Financial data + ridership data
- **Frequency:** Quarterly calculation
- **Benchmark:** Taipei ($0.035), mature metros ($0.03-0.04)

**Annual Operating Margin**
- **Definition:** Revenue − Operating costs (surplus/deficit)
- **Target:** +$50-100M annually (mature operations)
- **Measurement:** Annual financial statements
- **Frequency:** Quarterly tracking, annual reporting
- **Benchmark:** Profitable metros $50-200M annual surplus

**Debt Service Coverage Ratio**
- **Definition:** Annual operating surplus ÷ Annual debt service
- **Target:** >1.5x (can cover debt 1.5x over)
- **Measurement:** Financial accounting
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Benchmark:** Investment-grade requirement 1.25x+

**Capital Cost per km**
- **Definition:** Total capital cost ÷ Line length
- **Target:** $300-600M per km (completed cost)
- **Measurement:** Final project accounting
- **Frequency:** Post-project review
- **Benchmark:** Taipei ($403M/km), Bangkok estimate ($375M/km)

---

### Ridership KPIs

**Daily Ridership**
- **Definition:** Total passenger trips per 24 hours
- **Target:** 300,000 (or actual projection; ramp-up curve)
- **Measurement:** Farebox + smart card data
- **Frequency:** Daily counting, monthly summary
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (320,000), Beijing Ring 2 (400,000+)

**Ridership Growth Rate**
- **Definition:** % increase in ridership year-over-year
- **Target:** Year 1→2: +30%, Year 2→3: +15%, Year 3+: <5% (mature)
- **Measurement:** Monthly ridership comparison
- **Frequency:** Monthly tracking
- **Benchmark:** Most metros 20-50% growth Year 1-2, then 2-5%

**Mode Shift (Cars to Transit)**
- **Definition:** % of trips shifted from private cars to metro
- **Target:** 40% of metro riders former car drivers
- **Measurement:** Passenger survey (mode of previous trip)
- **Frequency:** Annual survey
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (45%), typical new metro (35-50%)

**Peak Hour Concentration**
- **Definition:** % of daily ridership in peak hour
- **Target:** 8-12% of daily (symmetric, unlike radial lines)
- **Measurement:** Hourly ridership data
- **Frequency:** Monthly analysis
- **Benchmark:** Circular lines 8-12%, radial lines 12-18%

**Catchment Population Served**
- **Definition:** % of metro area population within 1 km of station
- **Target:** ≥80%
- **Measurement:** GIS analysis of population distribution vs. station locations
- **Frequency:** Baseline at opening, then annual
- **Benchmark:** Well-designed metros 75-90%

---

### Environmental KPIs

**CO₂ Emissions Avoided**
- **Definition:** Metric tons CO₂ avoided annually vs. baseline (if traveled by car)
- **Target:** 40,000+ tons/year (typical for 300k daily riders)
- **Measurement:** Ridership × distance × emissions factor
- **Frequency:** Annual calculation
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (40,000+ tons/year), typical metro (30-60k tons)

**Air Quality Improvement (PM2.5)**
- **Definition:** % reduction in particulate matter in corridor zone
- **Target:** 15-20% reduction near line
- **Measurement:** Air quality monitoring stations
- **Frequency:** Annual comparison
- **Benchmark:** New metro corridors 15-25% PM2.5 reduction

**Energy Consumption**
- **Definition:** kWh per passenger-km
- **Target:** <0.08 kWh per passenger-km
- **Measurement:** Energy metering system
- **Frequency:** Monthly tracking, annual reporting
- **Benchmark:** Taipei metro (<0.07), efficient metros (0.06-0.08)

**Green Space Created**
- **Definition:** Square meters of new parks/green space created around stations
- **Target:** 50+ hectares (station area development)
- **Measurement:** Landscape architecture records
- **Frequency:** Annual as development completes
- **Benchmark:** New metro corridors 30-100+ hectares

---

### Economic Impact KPIs

**Real Estate Value Appreciation**
- **Definition:** % increase in property value near stations
- **Target:** 15-25% appreciation within 500m of station (5-year cumulative)
- **Measurement:** Property valuation data
- **Frequency:** Annual property valuation
- **Benchmark:** Tokyo (20-30%), Taipei (20-25%), Hong Kong (25%+)

**Station Area Development**
- **Definition:** Square meters of new development within 500m of station
- **Target:** $5-10B private development over 10 years
- **Measurement:** Development permits + project records
- **Frequency:** Annual tracking
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (ongoing), Hong Kong MTR (8-12B HKD per line)

**Job Creation**
- **Definition:** Direct + indirect jobs created
- **Target:** 10,000+ construction jobs (6-year project), 200+ permanent jobs
- **Measurement:** Employment records
- **Frequency:** Annual tracking
- **Benchmark:** Taipei (12,000+ construction, 250+ permanent)

**Tax Revenue Generated**
- **Definition:** Property tax + income tax + sales tax from development
- **Target:** $100M+ annually from mature station area development
- **Measurement:** Tax authority records
- **Frequency:** Annual
- **Benchmark:** Varies by tax rate and development, typically 10-15% of property value annually

## Part 4: Success Criteria by Stakeholder

### For City Council / Government

**"Is this project a success?"**

Success = Meeting these targets:
- ✓ Operating profitably (farebox recovery ≥85-100%)
- ✓ Ridership growing toward projection (≥70% of Year 3 target by Year 2)
- ✓ Safety record excellent (zero fatalities, near-zero injuries)
- ✓ Economic development happening ($1B+ in Year 1, $5B+ in 5 years)
- ✓ Environmental impact realized (40,000+ tons CO₂/year)
- ✓ Public satisfied (≥70% positive rating)
- ✓ Political promise fulfilled (delivered on time/budget)

---

### For Transit Agency / Operators

**"Is this project operationally successful?"**

Success = Meeting these targets:
- ✓ Reliable operations (≥95% on-time, ≥99% availability)
- ✓ Safe operations (zero safety incidents, staff trained)
- ✓ Efficient operations (low cost per passenger-km)
- ✓ Staff satisfaction (≥75% satisfied with jobs)
- ✓ Customer satisfaction (≥75% satisfied with service)
- ✓ Integration with existing network seamless
- ✓ Maintenance budget on target
- ✓ Technology performing as designed

---

### For Passengers / Community

**"Is this project benefiting us?"**

Success = Meeting these targets:
- ✓ Reliable service (trains run on time, frequently)
- ✓ Affordable (fares reasonable, service worth the cost)
- ✓ Accessible (easy to reach stations, meet diverse needs)
- ✓ Clean (quality stations, safe environment)
- ✓ Fast (reduces commute time vs. cars/buses)
- ✓ Integrated (works with other transit)
- ✓ Benefits community (development, jobs, cleaner air)
- ✓ Responsive (operator listens and improves)

---

### For Funders / Investors

**"Is this project meeting financial targets?"**

Success = Meeting these targets:
- ✓ Loan repayments on schedule
- ✓ Farebox recovery ≥100% (covers all operating costs)
- ✓ Payback period tracking toward projection (10-15 years)
- ✓ No cost overruns or major disputes
- ✓ Project governance strong
- ✓ Risk management effective
- ✓ Financial reporting transparent
- ✓ Project on track for full repayment

## Part 5: Continuous Improvement

### Quarterly Review Process

**Every quarter, analyze:**

1. **What worked well?** (Keep doing)
   - Service reliability? Continue
   - Demand trends? Adjust marketing
   - Financial performance? Continue strategy
   - Staff performance? Reward and retain

2. **What needs improvement?** (Adjust)
   - Below-target on-time? Investigate and fix
   - Ridership below forecast? Marketing adjustment
   - Cost overruns? Process improvement
   - Customer complaints? Address root cause

3. **What's changed?** (Respond)
   - Market conditions?
   - Competition?
   - Political priorities?
   - Development patterns?

4. **What should we do next?** (Decide)
   - Service adjustments (frequency, hours, routes)?
   - Fare adjustments (discounts, passes)?
   - Marketing campaigns?
   - Infrastructure improvements?
   - Technology upgrades?

## Conclusion

**Success isn't luck. It's measured, tracked, and actively managed.**

By defining clear metrics and monitoring them rigorously, you:
- Know exactly where you stand at all times
- Catch problems early before they become critical
- Make data-driven decisions about adjustments
- Communicate clearly with stakeholders
- Ensure accountability for results

**The framework provided here is customizable to your specific context.** Adapt the metrics, targets, and benchmarks to match your city's goals and constraints.

**Key principle:** Measure what matters, report honestly, and act on what you learn.
