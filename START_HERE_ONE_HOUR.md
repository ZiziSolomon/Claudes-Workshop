### Step 2: Understand the Taipei Circular Line
The Taipei Metro Circular Line (opened 2020) serves as the reference:
- **Length:** 15.4 km
- **Stations:** 14 (1.1 km average spacing)
- **Daily ridership:** 320,000 passengers
- **Unique feature:** 100% driverless (GoA4 automation)
- **On-time performance:** 99.2%
- **Cost:** ~$6.2 billion (completed on budget, on schedule)

It's the world's first and only fully driverless rapid transit line—making it the best modern reference for cities planning similar systems.

## Quick Answers to Common Questions

**Q: Is a circular line right for my city?**
A: If metro area is 5M+, sprawling pattern, multiple employment centers, and 200k+ daily riders projected — probably yes. Use worksheet in `IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md` to assess.

**Q: How much does this cost?**
A: $400M-600M per km, or $12B-18B for a typical 30 km line. See `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` Part 2 and `IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md` cost worksheet.

**Q: How long to build?**
A: Planning 2 years + Design 2 years + Construction 4-5 years + Ramp-up 5 years = 13-14 years total. Can be faster in developed economies with experienced teams.

**Q: Is driverless automation necessary?**
A: No, but it improves economics 30-40% (reduces payback period by 5-10 years). Recommended if ridership >200k/day. See `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` Part 3.

**Q: How much ridership should I expect?**
A: Conservative estimate: (Catchment population × 15%) × 40% mode shift. Typical range: 150k-400k/day. Use forecasting worksheet in `IMPLEMENTATION_CHECKLIST.md`.

**Q: What's the payback period?**
A: Typically 10-25 years for 200k-300k daily riders. Shorter with automation, longer in developing economies. Calculate using economic model tool.

**Q: Which cities have circular lines?**
A: Moscow (1952), Beijing (1987+), Shanghai (1997), Taipei (2020). See `circular-metro-comparison.html` and `circular-metro-timeline.html`.

**Q: My city doesn't have a metro yet. Can we skip to circular?**
A: Not recommended. Radial lines first (core coverage), then add circular after 200k+ daily riders established. See `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` Part 4.
