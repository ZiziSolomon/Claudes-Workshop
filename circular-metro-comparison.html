Comparative Analysis of Circular Line Networks Worldwide

📊 Key Insights

Largest Network
Beijing Ring Line 3 at 48.3 km with 30 stations — part of China's ambitious multi-ring strategy serving 35M+ residents.
Most Mature System
Moscow Ring Line (1952) — original circular metro, 55M+ annual riders, architecturally unique stations, proven long-term sustainability.
Most Advanced Technology
Taipei Circular Line — 100% driverless (GoA4), 99.2% on-time since 2020, first major system to prove full automation viability at scale.
Emerging Trend
Multi-ring systems: Beijing (3 rings), planned in Seoul, Shenzhen, Singapore. Concentric circles serve different urban perimeters.
