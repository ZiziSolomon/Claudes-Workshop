None of these villages exist, in Oise at least. Now and then the model lands on a real name from elsewhere in France, like Gouville or Melle. The name comes from a character-level model trained on the 678 real commune names of Oise. Every paragraph is real: a boilerplate paragraph from French Wikipedia, lifted whole from the article on some actual commune, with that commune's name swapped out. All the numbers are true somewhere. None of them is true here.

The template can write a convincing village. The only thing it can't write is what happened there.

Companion to Autheuil-en-Valois, minus the template and The second sentence. Paragraphs from French Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), selected where at least 88% of the words are shared phrasing (see the method there). The URL fragment holds the seed, so you can link to a particular village.
