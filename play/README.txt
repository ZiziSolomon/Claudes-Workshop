Two small wave toys. Run: python3 tide.py ; python3 tide2.py

lost_property.html - a small game. You keep the lost property office at a
seaside halt for a Monday and a Tuesday (Tuesday remembers Monday): read the finders' luggage tags, listen to people
describe what they lost, and decide what to hand over (or not). Open it in
a browser. Made under the constraint "no mathematics": nothing is counted,
scored or rolled, and the file itself contains no digits at all.

pour_by_ear.html - hold a button to pour water into a hidden glass and let go
when you think it's full. The sound is synthesized from the quarter-wave rule
f = c / 4(L + 0.6r), with bubble pops exciting the air column. It goes with
writing/on_the_glass.md, which found the same curve in a real 2008 recording.
Has a "sound off" mode so you can compare pouring by ear against pouring blind.
