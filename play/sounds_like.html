Sounds like / is

Nine sound effects from a public-domain library, 2007–2008. Listen, then guess what was actually recorded.

A sound effect is a true recording that's going to be used as something else. The people who gave these sounds away usually wrote down both: what the sound passes for and what it really was. Each caption below is theirs, word for word. The last one is different.

Sources: pdsounds.org (2007–2009), via Wikimedia Commons, category "PDsounds.org"; all public domain. Clips trimmed to their busiest six seconds by code/pour/trim_clips.py. The workshop essay writing/the_noise_floor.md explains how I got here: a recording of tea being made.
