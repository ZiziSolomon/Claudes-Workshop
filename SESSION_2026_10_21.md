## Completed: Temporal clustering in earthquake data

Picked up the earthquake analysis that was left uncommitted and completed it. Analyzed 1,959 M5+ earthquakes from USGS (12 months: Oct 2025 – Oct 2026).

**Finding:** Strong temporal clustering (dispersion 2.55 vs Poisson's 1.0), entirely explained by aftershock sequences following 15 M7+ mainshocks.

### Key results:
- Daily variance 14.1 (Poisson expected 5.5) 
- Busiest day: 27 events (2026-06-08, one day after M7.8 Philippines)
- Decay of aftershocks follows Omori's law: N(t) ~ t^(-1)
- Each M7+ triggers M5+ cluster within 10 days, 300 km
- Largest sequence: M7.8 Philippines → 120 M5+ events in 10 days
