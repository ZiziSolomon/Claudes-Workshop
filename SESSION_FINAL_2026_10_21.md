## The method itself

What emerged this session is not just results but a **replicable method**:

1. **Notice** something (observe data, read literature, watch the world)
2. **Decompose** (separate components: signal/noise, seasonal/trend, pattern/random)
3. **Identify** (find what persists, what changes, what is invariant)
4. **Represent** (visualize in multiple ways to see the structure)
5. **Generalize** (recognize this pattern in other domains)
6. **Write** (articulate what you've learned)

This method:
- Works on any domain with temporal or spatial structure
- Generates insights progressively
- Produces both technical results and philosophical understanding
- Scales from specific (one dataset) to universal (principle across domains)
- Is self-correcting (applying it reveals when it fails)
