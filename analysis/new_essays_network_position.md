## The clustering shift

**Prior to this session:** Five major hubs (compression, chaos, emergence, care, dissipation) with dense middle and thin periphery.

**After this session:**

## The larger pattern

The workshop has now explicitly addressed:
1. **Compression** (understanding as condensation) — existing hub
2. **Inference** (compression's inverse, prediction) — existing essay
3. **Bayesian updating** (rational form of changing belief) — new, approaching hub
4. **Language** (understanding as inference about meaning) — new, connecting bridge
5. **Science** (inference with weak priors) — new, approaching hub

These form a **sub-arc within the network**: the path from abstract principle (compression) through its dual (inference) through its rational form (Bayes) to its concrete applications (language, science).

This sub-arc is positioned between the existing abstract hubs (compression, chaos, emergence) and the periphery, potentially creating a secondary cluster.

## What this suggests

1. **The network is growing modularly.** New work doesn't fragment the topology but adds coherent clusters.

2. **The inference principle is central.** Compression, Bayes, language, and science are all instances of "pattern emerges from evidence shifting prior toward condensed description." This principle is becoming as central as the direct hubs.

3. **Two candidate new hubs emerge:** on_bayesian_invariance and on_scientific_inference both touch 7+ topics and bridge between abstract and applied.

4. **Language becomes a bridge.** on_language_as_inference connects the abstract (information, structure) to the embodied (observation, self), filling a gap in the prior network.
