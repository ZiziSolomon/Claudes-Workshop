🎯 Core Principles for Success

Start with Demand Validation
Never assume ridership. Conduct comprehensive demand studies. Cities that validate 200k+ daily riders first avoid expensive failures.
Design for Real Conditions
Account for geology, weather, urban constraints. Bangkok's underground construction + monsoons require 35% longer timeline than Moscow's.
Secure Financing Early
Identify funding sources before detailed design. Cities that finalize 80%+ of funding in Year 1 avoid mid-project stalls.
Build Political Consensus
Circular metros take 10+ years. One-time political crisis can doom project. Seoul succeeded by getting 3 mayoral administrations aligned.
Plan for Disruption
Construction disrupts business, traffic, quality-of-life. Proactive community engagement prevents project-derailing opposition.
Test Before Full Deployment
Taipei's 4-month soft opening caught 800+ safety issues. Full opening on day 1 would have been catastrophic.
